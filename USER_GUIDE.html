What is Log Analyser?

It reads your .log files, groups repeating errors together, and shows them on a simple web page. Instead of 90 near-identical error lines, you see 1 problem that happened 90 times.

🧩Errors groupedNo more scrolling through duplicates.
🚦Clear statusSee what is New, Spiking or Known.
📍Where it happenedClass, method, file and line.
⏱️Response timesAverage, fastest and slowest per endpoint.
🔒It runs on your own computer. Logs never leave it, nothing goes to the cloud, and it only reads your logs. It never changes them.

Get started in 3 steps

About two minutes. Nothing else to install.

DownloadGet the zip for your computer from the releases page and extract it.
Start itWindows: double-click LogAnalyser.Api.exe.
Mac/Linux: run ./run.sh in Terminal.
Open itYour browser opens at http://localhost:5171. Sign up (the account stays on your computer).
⚠️Windows may say it "protected your PC" because the app is not code-signed. Click More info → Run anyway.

Set up your logs

You do this once. Tell the app what to watch (a project) and where the logs are (an environment).

Your logs must look like this

  • Files end in .log.
  • Each line starts like 2025-01-31 14:05:09,123 -- ERROR -- service-name -- message
  • Each service has its own folder. Sub-folders are fine.

1. Create a project

A project is one application. Click New project, name it, and add one row per service: the folder name of its logs, without a prefix like dev-.

2. Add an environment

An environment is one place the logs live, such as dev or production. Open your project and click New environment.

Example. Your logs are in:
\\server\AKS-Dev-Logs\business-clm\dev-business-clm-analytics\ClmAnalyticsWebService
WhereFieldValue
ProjectServicebusiness-clm-analytics
EnvironmentNamedev
EnvironmentLog path\\server\AKS-Dev-Logs\business-clm
EnvironmentFolder prefixdev-
The app joins log path + prefix + service, then reads every .log file below it, including sub-folders like ClmAnalyticsWebService. You do not type the sub-folder.

After saving, wait a few minutes. The app re-checks by itself every 10 minutes by default. You can change this to 2, 5, 7 or 10 minutes (see How often it checks).

Read your dashboard

Click Open on an environment. You get key numbers, charts, and a table with one row per problem.

  • Search by message, service or class, and sort by any column.
  • Tick Show all to include everything. Otherwise you see only New and Spiking.
  • Click a row to see its details.

How often it checks

By default the app looks for new log lines every 10 minutes. You choose how fresh you want your data.

Click your name in the top bar and use Check logs every to pick:

2 minutes 5 minutes 7 minutes 10 minutes

It saves right away and applies within seconds.

💡Shorter means fresher data, but the app reads your log share more often.

Stop and restart

Click your name in the top bar, then Quit Log Analyser. (Mac/Linux: you can also press Ctrl+C in Terminal.) To start again, open the exe (or run.sh) as before. Your data is kept.

Common questions

Nothing shows up. Why?

Check, in order: the environment card for cannot read log files; the log path and prefix; that files end in .log; that lines match the format above; and that your logs contain ERROR or WARN lines. Then wait a few minutes.

How fresh is the data?

Every 10 minutes by default. You can choose 2, 5, 7 or 10 minutes on your account page. It is not real time.

Does it change my logs?

No. It only reads them.

Where is my data stored?

Next to the program, in app.db and the environments/ folder. Keep them when you update.

How do I update?

Download the new zip and replace only the program file (LogAnalyser.Api.exe, or LogAnalyser.Api and run.sh). Leave config.json, app.db and environments/ alone.
